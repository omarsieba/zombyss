The goal

You start in a haunted wizard town with the other players. Zombies keep rising anywhere in town, and one touch from a zombie infects you. There are two ways to win:

🏆 Last survivorBe the last one alive (or the last team in Duo and Team). The round ends the moment only one remains.

Everyone who falls comes back as a zombie and hunts the players who are left.

A round, minute by minute

  1. 0:00Everyone starts together with no zombies around. Then one rises every 5 seconds, anywhere on the map, and a red circle on the ground warns you where. Treasure chests and supply crates appear around the map.
  2. 1:00The bounty starts. The player with the most points glows gold with a crown on every map. Zombies hunt them harder.
  3. 2:00The dead start rising faster.
  4. 2:30The Grave King climbs out of a crypt: a giant zombie that cannot be killed. A red circle shows where he is about to slam. Get out of it.

Zombies

  • A zombie infects you the moment it gets within arm's length, even if you are running past. Only armor or an untouchable skill saves you.
  • When several players stand together, the horde splits up and goes for all of them.
  • Zombies are slower than you, but they never get tired. Sprinting runs out of stamina.
  • Zombies with a name above them used to be a player or a bot.

If you fall

  • 3 seconds later you rise as a zombie. You have no health. Q/E lunge forward.
  • Catch a survivor to infect them: +40 points, or +120 if they are the bounty.
  • Predict the winner: pick the survivor you think will win. If you are right you get +15 rank points.
  • Possess the Grave King: when he is out, one zombie player at a time can control him for 30 seconds. Every survivor his slam hits counts as your infection.

Chests and crates

❤️ Medkit+1 heart
⚡ Energy drinkSkills and stamina refilled
🛡 ArmorBlocks the next bite

A treasure chest gives one magic item. You can carry one at a time. Use it with R or the item button.

Heroes

Pick your hero in the menu or the lobby. Every hero has two skills with a cooldown, and some have more hearts while others are faster.

Rank points (RP) only change in Ranked matches: 1st place +28, 2nd +16, 3rd +9, the rest of the top half +3. Finish in the bottom half and you lose 6 to 15 RP (more at Diamond and Master). A correct prediction adds +10, and as a zombie every survivor you infect adds +3 (up to 10 a match). Every infection also counts on the season's Top infectors board.

Seasons: a season lasts one calendar month. When a new one starts, everyone's rank points are halved. Diamond and Master players lose 20 RP a day after 7 days without playing.

Rank rewards: Silver unlocks a spark trail, Gold an arcane aura, Diamond a spirit owl that follows you, Master a jewelled crown. Turn them on in the menu.

Missions and hero mastery

  • Missions: 3 new daily missions every day and 3 weekly missions every week, the same for everyone. Daily ones give 120 hero XP, weekly ones 300. They count in every mode. Find them on the main menu.
  • Mastery: every round gives XP to the hero you played. Level 3 adds a star to your name, level 5 a golden ring, level 7 a spark trail, level 10 a legend glow.

Ways to play

▶ NormalPress Normal, invite friends with the party code, and the leader presses Find match. You are matched with other players into a 20-survivor game. No rank points, just fun.
🏆 RankedThe same, but you win or lose rank points and climb from Bronze to Master. Sign in to play ranked.
Solo, Duo, TeamThe party leader picks the mode. In Solo everyone is on their own. In Duo (2) and Team (4) your party plays together, a Priestess heals her teammates, and the last team standing wins.
Custom roomsPick your own room code, choose how many bots, and play with friends. No rank points.
🌙 Daily challengeThe same map for everyone today. Survive as long as you can. Your best time goes on today's leaderboard.
🏆 TournamentsScheduled events with real prizes. When one is live, sign in and press Join on the menu. Your best score counts.

Tips

  • Keep moving. A zombie that reaches you bites instantly.
  • Save a dash or escape skill for the moment you get cornered.
  • Toss a decoy behind you when the horde gets close: every zombie within 12 m chases it for 5 seconds.
  • As the bounty, run toward open ground. Every zombie on the map is coming for you.
  • Create an account so your rank, missions and mastery follow you to any phone or computer.
