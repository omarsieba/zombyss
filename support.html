Support

Something not working, or a question about your account? Email us at the contact address on this site and include your username and what device you play on. We usually answer within two working days.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, press Forgot your password? and enter your username or email. If your account has an email address, you will get a reset link that works for one hour.

My friend cannot join my room

Make sure you both typed the same room code, or send the invite with the WhatsApp button in the lobby. Some strict office or school networks block online games; try mobile data.

The game is slow on my phone

Close other apps and browser tabs. Older phones run the game best in the app version, or with fewer bots in the lobby.

How do I delete my account?

Sign in, open the main menu and press Delete account, then confirm with your password. Everything is removed straight away. See the privacy policy for details.

How long do I wait for a match?

Never more than about 20 seconds. Normal and Ranked matches always have 20 survivors: when not enough people are online, the empty places are filled with computer-controlled survivors.
