Privacy policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}. Zombyss is run by {{OWNER}} ({{SITE}}).

This page explains what Zombyss stores about you, why, and how to delete it. Zombyss has no ads, does not track you across other websites or apps, and does not sell your data.

If you play without an account

Your name, chosen hero, rank and settings are saved only on your own device (in the browser's or app's local storage). Nothing about you is stored on our server.

If you create an account

  • Username, display name and password. Your password is stored only as a secure hash; nobody can read it.
  • Email address, if you add one. It is used only to send you a password-reset link when you ask for one.
  • Game progress: rank points, season results, missions, hero mastery, cosmetics, your best daily-challenge times and tournament scores.

Your display name, hero and scores appear on public leaderboards and to other players in your matches.

Online matches

Online rooms connect players' devices directly to each other (peer to peer). This means other players in your room can technically see your device's IP address, as with most online games. Our server only helps players find each other and does not record your matches.

Technical data

Like every website, our server receives your IP address and basic device information when you connect. We use this only to deliver the game and to block abuse (for example too many sign-in attempts), and we do not build profiles from it.

Cookies and local storage

If you sign in on the website, a single cookie keeps you signed in. The apps use a sign-in token stored on your device instead. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

Children

Zombyss is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account until you delete it. You can delete it at any time inside the game: open the main menu, choose Delete account and confirm with your password. This permanently removes your account, progress and leaderboard entries. You can also ask us to delete it by email.

Your rights
