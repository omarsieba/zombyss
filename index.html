A round in Hollowmere

Every round starts with everyone together in the town square. This is what is coming for you.

  1. 0:00

    The dead rise

    A zombie climbs out of the ground every five seconds, anywhere in town. Grab chests for magic items.

  2. 1:00

    A price on your head

    The leading survivor becomes the bounty. Every zombie hunts them first.

  3. 1:30

    One touch turns you

    Zombies run you down without stopping. A single touch and you are one of them.

  4. 2:00

    The horde grows

    The dead rise faster. Every decoy and skill counts now.

  5. 2:30

    The Grave King

    A giant that cannot be killed climbs out of his crypt. Do not be under his fist.

Questions

Is Zombyss free?

Yes. The whole game is free, with no ads.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs in the browser on phones, tablets and computers. Apps for Android and iPhone are coming.

Do I need an account?

Not for Normal matches. Ranked and Tournaments need a free account so your rank and scores are saved on every device.

How long do I wait for a match?

Never more than about 20 seconds. Every Normal and Ranked match has 20 survivors.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes. Press Normal or Ranked, share your party code, and the leader presses Find match. Up to four friends go in together. You can also make a custom room with your own rules.

How do I learn the controls?

The game shows a short guide the first time you play, and the full how-to-play guide explains everything.
